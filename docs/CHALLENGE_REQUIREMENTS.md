# Official challenge requirements

**Checked 11 November 2026** against the [HKU SPH One Health Team Challenge 2026–2027 page](https://s.sph.hku.hk/theonehealthhub/team-challenge/), including its timeline, submission section and FAQ. This is a dated summary, not a registration, submission or acceptance of funding terms. Recheck the official page before acting.

## What the team must deliver

The challenge calls for realistic, student-led **community engagement micro-campaigns or short-term activities**, not research alone. Choose one theme: AMR and everyday safety; emerging infections and zoonotic risks; or climate change and health awareness.

- Teams: 3–5 eligible students, with one team leader. Senior secondary and undergraduate students are eligible.
- Team registration closes **30 November 2026, 09:00 HKT**. The separate **17 November** milestone concerns reserved seats at the 24 November film screening, not the final team-registration deadline.
- Proposal and prototype are due **18 January 2027, 23:59 HKT**.
- Submit an **English PDF of at most five pages**: title, audience, theme, problem/solution, execution plan, February–June 2027 timeline, reach/impact/evaluation and itemised budget.
- Include **at least one campaign-material prototype**. Digital mockups, scripts and interactive-game prototypes are acceptable formats. Campaign materials can be Traditional Chinese, English or bilingual; proposals and pitches are in English.

## Published judging weights

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---:|
| One Health alignment and relevance | 25% |
| Innovation and creativity | 25% |
| Community engagement and impact | 25% |
| Feasibility and execution plan | 15% |
| Prototype quality | 10% |

These are official weights. The comparisons in the [team brief](TEAM_BRIEF.md) remain our own decision framework, not official scores or a selected project.

## Commitments to check before entry or funding

The FAQ expects at least two team representatives at the workshop and screening; notify the organiser if the entire team cannot attend. Winning teams may receive up to **HKD 10,000**, subject to approved budgets, and implement during February–June 2027. Accepting funding grants the Hub educational/publicity showcase rights. Post-project requirements include a short video, testimonials and a report of at most three pages.
