# Starting with another collaborator

The research, code and current decisions belong in this repository so a new collaborator can continue from evidence. Neither the current demo nor the shortlist is a commitment to a final product.

Read [the companion guide](COMPANION_GUIDE.md) first for a plain-language walkthrough and the scientific, design and adoption perspectives we can consult. Explain the human situation and the actual behavior before describing technical capabilities.

## Opus — investigate and build

> We're exploring an ambitious One Health challenge project. Read AGENTS.md, docs/TEAM_BRIEF.md, docs/DECISIONS.md and docs/STARTER_TASKS.md. Help us find a workflow where AI changes what a person can actually do. You have freedom to question the shortlist, connect mechanisms from older hackathons, and propose a stronger direction. Choose one consequential assumption, explain it through a concrete human scene, and make the smallest complete experiment that tests it. Build on a focused branch, preserve source attribution, run the relevant checks and leave an experiment note. Keep observed results distinct from proposals. We want something a teammate can understand, try and challenge.

## Fable — make the idea understandable

> Read AGENTS.md and docs/TEAM_BRIEF.md, then choose a direction you find worth exploring. Start with a person and a moment where something matters. Give us one complete encounter that helps a newcomer understand the problem, experience a consequential choice and see what changed. You have creative freedom over the form, rhythm and point of view. Tell us what evidence or assets you need; mark fictional scenarios and schematic imagery clearly. The existing Follow the Resistance explorer is an experiment you may reinterpret, not a template you must extend. Leave the experience playable or reviewable, with a short note on the understanding it aims to create and how we could test that with people.

## A first teammate contribution

Read the six scenes in the team brief. Pick one you can explain in your own words and one assumption you doubt. Add an observation, source or proposed test with `contributions/TEMPLATE.md`. We can choose two experiments at the meeting; choosing what to test is different from committing the whole team to a product.

## Handoff back to the team

Give the branch/PR, an opening link or command, what actually works, the evidence behind it, and the next decision. Keep the explanation short enough for someone to try it before a meeting. Put detailed reasoning beside the artifact, not in every screen of the experience.
