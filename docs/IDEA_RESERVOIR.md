# The pre-generative-AI idea reservoir

Research snapshot: 9 November 2026. These are design ancestors, not endorsements. Several already used machine learning. A prize, prototype, deployed service and measured health benefit are different kinds of evidence. No historical code was copied or run.

## HandyMap - HackMIT 2019

- Mechanism: A directory becomes directions to the specific accessible entrance.
- Evidence: Creator-reported Android demo; no field-effectiveness evaluation found.
- Our proposed adaptation: CoolDoor: turn official cooling-space notices and verified entrance photos into usable bilingual arrival instructions. AI extracts changes and flags conflicts; verified records determine routing.
- First test: Audit ten venues; compare finding the entrance and checking availability against the official listing.
- Primary source: https://devpost.com/software/handymap

## PillPack - MIT Hacking Medicine 2012

- Mechanism: Medication management becomes a coordinated service of sorted packets, delivery and refills.
- Evidence: Deployed in 2014; MIT reported more than one million packets shipped by August 2015. This does not validate our adaptation.
- Our proposed adaptation: Prescription teach-back: a pharmacist confirms the instructions extracted from a label, then the patient hears an explanation and explains it back. AI handles language and questions; the pharmacist approves the plan.
- First test: Compare comprehension and pharmacist correction time against a standard leaflet.
- Primary source: https://mitsloan.mit.edu/ideas-made-to-matter/a-redesigned-pharmacy-pillpack-gains-traction

## Dogemon - IMED 2016, supported by MIT Hacking Medicine

- Mechanism: A community dog sighting is routed to a veterinarian for a possible vaccination follow-up.
- Evidence: Published prototype concept for Indonesia; no deployment or disease reduction established here.
- Our proposed adaptation: A locally needed animal-observation reporting workflow: photo/voice input, completeness checks, duplicate grouping, expert review and feedback. No diagnosis or vaccination inference from a photo.
- First test: Find an actual recipient first; compare completeness of mock reports and review time.
- Primary source: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijid.2017.09.025

## ProMED Mapper - IMED 2016

- Mechanism: Related outbreak reports become easier to find and inspect.
- Evidence: Organizer paper describes a prototype grouping more than 53,000 reports; operational benefit not established.
- Our proposed adaptation: AMR Evidence Desk: extract study methods, locate supporting passages, expose disagreements and assemble a reviewable brief for an SPH educator or researcher.
- First test: Time a real evidence task and count unsupported claims versus ordinary searching. Do not assume the historic ProMED corpus is freely reusable today.
- Primary source: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijid.2017.09.025

## Footprint - IMED 2016

- Mechanism: Humanitarian logistics records become an environmental-impact assessment.
- Evidence: Accelerator reported public beta and Iraq field testing in 2017; emissions reduction was not established.
- Our proposed adaptation: A small clinic carbon audit: AI extracts utility/procurement quantities; a versioned calculator applies documented factors; a manager compares practical changes.
- First test: Reconcile one manual audit and measure staff time and actionable decisions.
- Primary source: https://medium.com/@sektor5/impact-start-up-footprint-launches-its-carbon-accounting-platform-in-public-beta-d253b7e5c95

## FreshDetect - HackMIT 2019

- Mechanism: A change in a shipment condition triggers reassignment to another buyer.
- Evidence: Cardboard-container hardware demo and creator-reported model metrics; no food-safety or field-outcome validation established.
- Our proposed adaptation: Food-rescue dispatch: parse donation messages, dates and quantities; match collection windows and recipient capacity; draft a plan for NGO review.
- First test: Replay 20-30 offers with a coordinator; measure feasible matches, review time and incorrect suggestions. Images cannot certify food safety.
- Primary source: https://devpost.com/software/fresh-detect

## LabelLearn - HackMIT 2019

- Mechanism: Model suggestions and previous labels give a human annotator context.
- Evidence: Creators report a working model pipeline; improved annotation consistency was a hypothesis.
- Our proposed adaptation: Use a human review queue for ambiguous AMR metadata: specimen, date, selection procedure, assay and denominator.
- First test: Blind-check 30-50 extracted records against manual review, including missing and incompatible fields.
- Primary source: https://devpost.com/software/doway

## Eye in the Sky - HackMIT 2019

- Mechanism: Official weather signals and local community observations share a map.
- Evidence: Android prototype. Its creator says automated image tagging was demoed locally but not integrated; verification was future work.
- Our proposed adaptation: Borrow the report-and-verification workflow for cooling access or a school caretaker inspection queue.
- First test: Include duplicates, stale reports and vague locations; compare actionable tasks and review time.
- Primary source: https://devpost.com/software/eye-in-the-sky-nm1l3k

## Water Crusader - HackMIT 2019

- Mechanism: Public context and fresh local observations identify questions for investigation.
- Evidence: The sensor was a temperature/humidity simulation, not a lead detector; no validated water-risk product established.
- Our proposed adaptation: Sampling Gap Explorer: harmonise surveillance metadata and expose missing places, seasons or specimen types for a research partner.
- First test: Ask a microbiologist whether apparent gaps survive methodological review. Never infer AMR from poverty or temperature proxies.
- Primary source: https://devpost.com/software/water-crusader

## Reuse notes

HandyMap's repository has an MIT licence; its old Android implementation has not been executed here. Eye in the Sky has conflicting MIT/Apache licence signals. No licence was detected for the inspected FreshDetect, LabelLearn and Water Crusader repositories. Borrow the mechanism; resolve terms before copying code.
